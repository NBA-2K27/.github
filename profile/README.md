## What Is NBA 2K27?

NBA 2K27 is a basketball simulation developed by Visual Concepts and published by 2K. The game combines NBA and WNBA basketball, player development, team management, card collection, and competitive matchups across several distinct game modes.

Players can create a custom athlete, build a roster of basketball stars, manage a franchise, or take part in matches inspired by professional basketball. The experience includes updated player animations, refined shooting mechanics, defensive adjustments, and new ways to customize a player's style.

The game introduces a co-ed City experience, allowing players to explore new basketball environments and take part in activities across its courts. The City also features Crew HQ, The REC, and Park activities.

MyCAREER adds an Eras-based experience that lets players revisit iconic periods of basketball history. Its Fire & Concrete storyline introduces a narrative focused on ambition, competition, and the path toward basketball recognition.

NBA 2K27 also expands franchise management through MyNBA, where players can shape a basketball organization over a long-term simulation. MyTEAM offers a separate collection-based experience built around assembling lineups and completing challenges.

## Core Gameplay Features

### MyCAREER and MyPLAYER

Create a custom basketball player and develop a playing style around selected attributes, abilities, and equipment. Character development affects how the athlete performs on the court.

The MyPLAYER Builder offers additional customization options, allowing players to shape different roles and experiment with combinations of attributes and badges.

### MyCAREER Eras

The Eras mode brings historical basketball settings into MyCAREER. Players can engage with different periods of the sport while following a career-focused storyline.

Fire & Concrete adds a narrative centred on the player's rise through the basketball scene, connecting character development with matches and activities around the City.

### MyTEAM Collection Building

MyTEAM focuses on collecting basketball player cards and assembling a custom lineup. Players can build squads featuring NBA and WNBA athletes and test their selections in challenges and matchups.

The mode includes a redesigned interface, an updated Exchange system, card discovery tools, and rotating activities.

### MyNBA Franchise Management

MyNBA gives players control over a basketball franchise. Responsibilities include shaping the roster, making team decisions, and managing a long-term basketball organization.

The expanded 100-year universe allows extended franchise simulations, offering a broader view of how a team can develop over many seasons.

### The City and Street Basketball

The City provides a shared basketball environment with multiple courts and activities. Players can customize their athlete, join crews, and participate in different styles of court competition.

The co-ed City experience expands the basketball environment with new settings and opportunities to play alongside a wider range of athletes.

## Gameplay and Control Enhancements

### Shooting and Finishing

NBA 2K27 introduces gameplay adjustments designed to give players more control over scoring actions.

- A manually timed Dunk Meter adds timing-based input to dunk attempts.
- Expanded dribbling customization allows greater control over ball-handling style.
- Dynamic layups offer additional finishing options near the basket.
- Rhythm shooting provides another way to manage shot timing.

### Defensive Systems

Defensive gameplay receives adjustments to positioning, shot contests, and player responses. Defensive AI and coaching behaviour also receive changes intended to make team play more responsive.

Players must consider spacing, matchup assignments, defensive positioning, and timing when attempting to stop opposing scorers.

### Player Animations

ProPLAY technology translates real basketball movements into in-game animations. This supports movement patterns, shooting actions, and other details intended to make player behaviour resemble the sport.

### Team Tactics

Basketball remains a team-based game, and effective play depends on more than individual scoring. Passing, spacing, defensive assignments, substitutions, and lineup choices all influence match outcomes.
